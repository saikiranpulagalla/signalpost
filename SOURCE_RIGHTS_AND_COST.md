# Sources, rights, and costs

Current official-source path: public, read-only Brønnøysund Register Centre endpoints for entity identity, roles, subunits, group structure, and annual accounts. Use only within documented public endpoint terms, rate limits, and any Builderr-provided budget. Website/social acquisition remains quarantined and is not part of this release command.

Observed five-company pilot cost: USD 0; 25 physical BRREG requests. This is a measured small pilot, not a guarantee of 100-company throughput or cost. The nominal fully enriched planning ceiling is five source operations per company, or roughly 500 for 100 candidates; caps must be explicitly approved and passed to the run command.

For future authorised live runs, `--source-snapshots-dir` may persist only permitted public response bytes under a byte cap, with content-addressed SHA-256 verification. Do not store protected endpoints, credentials, or unnecessary personal data. Existing historical pilot raw bytes were not retained.
