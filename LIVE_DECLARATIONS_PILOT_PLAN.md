declarations, NOT observations of completed operations or dated news.

research projection is version4 when present. Legacy normalized values retain

`signalpost-live-declarations/2026-10-20`, actual JSON field pointers, exact
company URL, retrieval time and digest. Fresh live-bootstrap and frozen+live
enrichment both use the new normalizer. No scheduler/priority defaults changed.

A current declaration is published once rather than duplicated as frozen and
live claims; the frozen row stays inspectable. Distinct activity/purpose fields
do not imply independent operational evidence or guaranteed separate score credit.

comparable previous/current live declarations; missing current values do not
invent a deletion. Original evidence remains historical. Hostile text is data.
